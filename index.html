Justin Moore — Creator Wizard / Sponsor Magnet

The structural opposite of every funnel in this file. No paid traffic, no webinar, no VSL, no countdown theatre. A 27,000-person newsletter that delivers real sponsorship opportunities twice a week feeds a $27 four-day live challenge, which feeds a $12,000 coaching program sold by a two-branch application that gates on revenue already earned. The move worth studying: he killed his $3,000 course and made it a free bonus inside the coaching, leaving the full sales page live as a value stack.

What matters

He killed a $3,000 course to make his $12,000 offer heavier

Brand Deal Wizard used to be a $3,000 cohort course — his main product for years. The page is still live, still 27,000 characters, still 20+ Vimeo modules and 506 testimonials, and now its only outbound link is the coaching application. The first line of the page is “UPDATE: Access to Brand Deal Wizard is now FREE when you join our Sponsorship Coaching program!” He didn't delete the asset, he demoted it into the value stack of the offer above it. The whole sales page now works as proof of what the $12k includes.

The $27 ticket is a buyer filter, not a revenue line

$27 against $590 of stacked bonuses cannot be about margin. It does three things a free webinar can’t: it takes a card, it takes a phone number plus explicit SMS autodialer consent, and it makes the refund conditional on showing up to all four days with camera on. He is buying a list of people who have paid him once and can legally be called. This is a read, not his stated intent.

The refund condition IS the show-rate mechanism

“If you attend all the live sessions (with your camera on!), pay attention, put in the work, and don’t feel like you received a massive amount of value… full refund within 7 days.” The guarantee is only claimable by someone who attended everything. Nobody has to be nagged into showing up — the money does it. Replays also expire when the next day starts. Compare: we currently run a free class and fight for show rate with follow-up volume.

The lead magnet is a recurring deliverable, not a PDF

“Get brand sponsorship opportunities in your inbox every Monday and Thursday.” The subscriber has a reason to open twice a week forever, and the proof on the opt-in page puts a number on the free thing: “I have made over $17,000 from brand deals I found through Justin’s newsletter.” A PDF is consumed once. This compounds, and it is why 27,000 people is enough traffic to run the whole business.

The application is two branches and gates on cash already earned

Paperform ztbjvcrc, titled “Sponsorship Coaching Application (Organic)” — the word Organic means a paid-traffic version exists. Branch A opens “Have you negotiated a sponsorship in the last 30 days?” then asks deals closed in 12 months, sponsorship revenue AND total revenue in six bands from $0 to $200k+, largest deal ever, monthly inbound inquiries, audience size, and job situation. Branch B, for people who haven’t closed recently, swaps in the question A never gets: “Have you invested money into growing your business in the last 12 months? (coaching, courses, etc.)” If you can’t show revenue, he checks whether you have ever paid for help.

The price is a commitment question, not a disclosure

“If we could help you achieve your sponsorship goals and be in your corner with your pitches, pricing, negotiations, packages, and proposals, would it be well worth the $12k investment to work with us?” — Yes / No / Maybe. Immediately followed by “how soon would you want to get started? ASAP / 1–2 months / 3+ months / I’m not interested in coaching at all.” Price objection and timeline objection are both dead in writing before a human dials.

Zero paid traffic. The audience IS the acquisition channel.

Meta Pixel, GTM, GA and Hyros are all installed on the challenge page — he is instrumented to run paid — but an ad-library sweep returned no active Creator Wizard ads. Traffic is the newsletter, the podcast, the YouTube channel, a published book, an affiliate program paying 100% on the front end, and borrowed authority from Ali Abdaal and Jay Clouse. Keyword search of the ad library is not exhaustive; treat as evidence, not proof.

Proof is named people with public identities, never screenshots

Ali Abdaal (£200,000, ~2× sponsorship revenue), Jenny Hoyos ($15,000, with a full case-study page), Jenny Wood ($6,000 first deal off one email), Molly Donlan ($17,000 from newsletter opportunities). Zero income screenshots anywhere. Every claim is attached to a person you can go verify yourself, which is a harder asset to build and an unfalsifiable one to attack.

The event's prize is the product

Sponsor Games 2027 gives away $5,000 cash plus seven real $2,500 brand deals — funded by Lulu, Paperform and Kit. Attendees at a sponsorship event win actual sponsorships. The event proves the mechanism, and his sponsors pay the $17,500 prize pool. Tickets are on sale roughly 18 months out.

Day 3 of the challenge is unfakeable proof of mechanism

“We’ll select creators LIVE to share their dream brands, then I’ll show you exactly how to stalk those brands on social media to uncover the hidden clues that reveal what should be in your $10K package. You’ll watch me decode their marketing strategy in real-time.” Live diagnosis on a stranger’s real situation cannot be scripted, and it is the day nobody wants to catch on replay.
